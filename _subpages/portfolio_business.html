Business Development

I support organizations, startups, NGOs, and academic institutions by integrating strategy, research, and implementation to design, scale, and sustain high-impact initiatives.

Scope of Work

  • Market assessment and feasibility studies
  • Strategic planning and organizational growth frameworks
  • Proposal writing and concept note development
  • Grant writing and donor engagement support
  • Public–private partnership (PPP) facilitation
  • Research-informed business and policy advisory

Approach & Methodology

My work follows a structured, evidence-based process that prioritizes clarity, feasibility, and long-term institutional impact.

  1. Problem definition – identifying strategic, institutional, and market-level gaps
  2. Data and stakeholder analysis – combining qualitative insights with quantitative evidence
  3. Strategy development – designing practical, scalable solutions
  4. Implementation support – guidance on execution, documentation, and monitoring

Tools & Expertise

  • Market, policy, and institutional analysis
  • Financial appraisal and investment assessment
  • Impact indicators and performance measurement
  • Python-based data analysis and visualization
  • GIS and spatial decision-support systems
