Research & Data Analysis

I provide research, data analysis, and evidence-based insights to support policy, business, and environmental decision-making.

Scope of Services

  • Policy and market research
  • Data collection, cleaning, and processing
  • Statistical and econometric analysis
  • Spatial and GIS analysis
  • Python-based data modeling and visualization
  • Report writing and policy recommendations

Methodology

Research projects follow a structured, reproducible workflow that ensures data integrity, analytical rigor, and actionable insights.

  1. Problem framing – define research questions and objectives
  2. Data collection & validation – surveys, secondary data, and stakeholder inputs
  3. Analysis & modeling – statistical, econometric, and spatial techniques
  4. Reporting & recommendations – clear, actionable findings for decision-making

Tools & Expertise

  • Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, GeoPandas)
  • R and statistical modeling
  • GIS and spatial analysis tools (QGIS, ArcGIS)
  • Survey design and data collection platforms
  • Policy analysis and research reporting
