# SMK University Lithuania 🇱🇹 Admission Process – Step by Step

1. Provide all docs
* Apostilled X and XII certificates with mark sheets
* Passport copy
* Other necessary documents in colour scans (and translations if needed)
2. Motivational interview – Once your application is reviewed, you will be invited for an online interview.
3. Document recognition process – SMK verifies your academic qualifications (usually 1–4 weeks).
4. Conditional acceptance letter & invoice – If accepted, you will receive these by email.
5. Tuition fee payment – Pay the full first-year tuition (non-EU students needing a TRP must pay in full before documents are issued).
6. Mediation code for TRP application – Issued by SMK after payment.
7. Apply for Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) via the Lithuanian Migration Information System (MIGRIS).

Arrival in Lithuania – Attend orientation week, submit your original documents, and start your studies.
Tuition Fees for Non-EU Students: from €3,500/year (depending on study field).

We cannot confirm your eligibility until your full application and all required documents are submitted for evaluation. You may start your application.
