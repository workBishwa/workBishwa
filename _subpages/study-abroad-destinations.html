Study Abroad Destinations

Choosing the right destination is more than selecting a country — it’s about aligning education, lifestyle, cost, and long-term opportunities. Explore popular study destinations for Nepali students, with honest insights to help you decide wisely.

Australia

  • Key Advantages: World-class universities, large Nepali community, strong PR pathways
  • Key Considerations: Higher costs, competitive admission
  • Living Cost: AUD 1,300 – 1,800 / month
  • Post-Study Work: 2–4 years
Explore Australia

Canada

  • Key Advantages: Excellent PR routes, safe & multicultural society
  • Key Considerations: Permit caps, early application required
  • Living Cost: CAD 830 – 1,600 / month
  • Post-Study Work: Up to 3 years (PGWP)
Explore Canada

United States

  • Key Advantages: Top-ranked universities, strong STEM & research
  • Key Considerations: High cost, competitive visa process
  • Living Cost: USD 980 – 1,950 / month
  • Post-Study Work: 1–3 years (OPT)
Explore USA

United Kingdom

  • Key Advantages: Prestigious degrees, 1-year Master’s
  • Key Considerations: High costs, dependent visa limits
  • Living Cost: GBP 815 – 1,390 / month
  • Post-Study Work: 2 years (Graduate Route)
Explore UK

New Zealand

  • Key Advantages: Safe, student-friendly, affordable
  • Key Considerations: Smaller job market
  • Living Cost: NZD 520 – 885 / month
  • Post-Study Work: 1–3 years
Explore New Zealand

Japan

  • Key Advantages: Affordable, strong tech & engineering
  • Key Considerations: Language & cultural adjustment
  • Living Cost: Lower outside Tokyo
  • Post-Study Work: Work visa pathways available
Explore Japan

Germany

  • Key Advantages: Low/no tuition, strong job market
  • Key Considerations: Language, blocked account
  • Living Cost: €700 – 1,000 / month
  • Post-Study Work: 18-month job seeker visa
Explore Germany

Malaysia

  • Key Advantages: Most affordable, English-medium programs
  • Key Considerations: Limited post-study options
  • Living Cost: USD 300 – 500 / month
  • Post-Study Work: Internship-based pathways
Explore Malaysia
