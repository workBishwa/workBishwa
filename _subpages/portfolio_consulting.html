Consulting Projects

I provide research-driven consulting and advisory support to organizations, development partners, and academic institutions across policy, strategy, environment, and data-informed decision-making.

Areas of Consulting

  • Policy and institutional analysis
  • Environmental and social safeguards advisory
  • Project design, appraisal, and evaluation
  • Monitoring, evaluation, and learning (MEL)
  • Data analysis, modeling, and decision support
  • Research, reporting, and technical documentation

Consulting Approach

Each engagement is tailored to the client context, combining rigorous analysis with practical, implementable recommendations.

  1. Scoping & needs assessment – defining objectives and constraints
  2. Evidence gathering – data analysis, field inputs, and stakeholder consultation
  3. Analytical synthesis – translating evidence into actionable insights
  4. Delivery & support – reports, presentations, and advisory follow-up

Tools & Expertise

  • Policy and project appraisal frameworks
  • Quantitative and qualitative research methods
  • Python-based data analysis and visualization
  • GIS and spatial analysis tools
  • Monitoring indicators and evaluation matrices
