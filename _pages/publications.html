M.Sc. Thesis: Temperature Trend Analysis Along with Paddy Production Trend and Their Impact on Paddy Cultivation in Koshi Basin, Nepal

Primary Author & Researcher | **Degree:** M.Sc. Environmental Science

In this study, Mann-Kendal test was used for trend analysis and ordinary least square regression method was used for relationship analysis. Study showed for annual indices two significant result TNn in mountain is increasing and TXx in hill is decreasing in contrary except TXx in terai and TNx and TXx in hills showed insignificant but increasing trend. For seasonal trend 75% of variable showed the positive trend of temperature indices and for monthly temperature more than 68% of variable showed positive trend of temperature indices. Only in the hill region regression model; showed significant result explaining 11.5% of variation in paddy yield. Regression model of total cropland area and paddy cultivation area showed the significant result for explaining paddy cultivation area using cropland of koshi basin model explains the 26.7% of variation in cultivation area. Study underscores the importance of considering various factors beyond temperature indices alone for explaining paddy yield in regions.
